Evidence before opinion

The one rule that separates this from “ask an AI about a business.” Every finding is tagged and sourced.
VerifiedDirectly observed in a source — with URL, excerpt, timestamp.
InferenceStrongly implied by evidence, but not stated outright.
HypothesisA lead worth checking — never presented as fact.

What the engine runs

Deterministic backend, AI on top — never the reverse.
Multi-relationship scoring
A business isn’t just a lead — it may be a referral, partner, or vendor. All scored separately.
Product match from catalog
The AI recommends — it never invents pricing. Every price comes from the XPI catalog.
Guardrails, not autonomy
Agents research and draft. Sending, buying, and deleting stay behind human approval.
